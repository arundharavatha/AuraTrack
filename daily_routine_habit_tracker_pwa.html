How to Install on Phone & System

AuraTrack is configured as a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA). When installed, it launches like a native program without the URL bar and works offline.

Windows / Mac / Chrome

1. Look at your browser address bar on the right side.

2. Click the Install Icon (⊕ or 💻) or click Install App in the header.

3. It will be added to your Desktop / Applications and run on startup.

Android & iPhone

Chrome (Android): Tap browser menu (⋮) → tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install app".

Safari (iOS): Tap the Share button (square with arrow) → scroll down & select "Add to Home Screen".

💡 Pro Tip: Once installed on your phone or PC, all your streaks, reminders, and timers continue to run even without an active internet connection.
